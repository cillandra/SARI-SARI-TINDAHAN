## PART 1: HOW THE GAME WORKS (OVERVIEW)
Tindahan is a 2D, mouse-driven, cozy management game about running a Filipino sari-sari store. Each in-game day has an untimed preparation phase, about 8 minutes of timed service, and an untimed report. During service, neighbors queue at the window (cap 6). Each customer has either a retail order (1 to 3 lines: pieces, measured bulk goods, or a peso amount) or one wallet service (cash-in from Day 4, cash-out from Day 6). The player picks the goods, measures bulk items, takes payment, makes exact change from a limited drawer, and optionally grants credit (utang, from Day 5) to eligible regulars. Money, stock, reputation, debts and the wallet persist between days. Customer patience keeps running in every view. The goal is to keep the shop solvent, keep reputation up, and grow profit across the ten introductory days and then endless play (Day 11+ uses a seeded rotation with capped rushes). Day 10 never ends the run. Low-money states are always recoverable (retry the day from its morning snapshot, or a new run).

- TIER 2: Utang (Day 5), Phone wallet (cash-in Day 4, cash-out Day 6, outage Day 9), Preparation screen, report corkboard, Days 6 to 10 situations, recovery screen.
- TIER 3: extra polish, optional art additions, balance pass.

## PART 2: THE SEQUENCES (implement and test exactly these flows)
A. APP LIFECYCLE
Boot -> loading screen (preload assets, procedural caches) -> Title (New / Continue; Continue dimmed with tooltip if no valid save) -> Preparation (Day N) -> Open store -> Service -> Close -> Report -> auto-save -> Preparation (Day N+1). Pause (Escape or hamburger icon) is available everywhere and suspends ALL simulation time. A corrupt or missing save shows a readable message with backup recovery or a new game.
B. DAY TIMELINE
Preparation (untimed: buy stock, set prices, exchange coins, prepack, wallet load/withdraw if unlocked, ice on brownout days, read the day's briefing) -> "Buksan ang tindahan" starts service -> customers spawn from the seeded generator while the queue is under the cap -> at closing time arrivals stop, remaining customers are finished or lost -> report (revenue, cost of goods, operating costs, spoilage, change losses, unpaid credit, abandoned customers, cash flow and profit separate, reputation) -> save -> next day. Day 1 to 10 situations and the Day 11+ rotation come from data/days.lua.
C. RETAIL SALE
1. Customer arrives (Customers.spawn), joins the queue; the front customer's patience ring starts.
2. Player clicks the customer: order bubble appears (items, quantities, measured goods, peso amounts); optional quirky mix-up with A/B replies (affects mood and tip only).
3. Player gathers items: packaged goods from the Shelves cabinet (Retail.add with reservation via Inventory.reserve); bulk goods through the Timbangan window; the basket sits on the counter with quantity badges.
4. Player opens Sukli (cash drawer prop): sees Total and Tender only; takes the customer's tender (Retail.stageTender).
5. Player selects change from the drawer's real denominations; confirm commits ONCE (Retail.commit): revenue and cost of goods are recorded; stock is consumed; reputation and mood update; the customer leaves happy.
6. Failure branches: wrong change -> complaint toast and correction step (excess change = recorded loss); impossible change -> cancel/refund with a reputation cost; patience reaches zero in ANY view -> customer leaves, Retail.cancel releases every reservation and staged cash, bounded reputation penalty; repeated clicks never commit twice.
D. WEIGH (Bigas, in the Timbangan window)
Player clicks the timbangan (counter) -> Timbangan window opens in repack mode with the product the order needs selected -> clicks Bigas in the choice bar -> weigh mode: click the sack to add a scoop (Portions.adjust +), click the pan to remove one, watch the needle and tolerance bar -> Portions.confirm only inside tolerance (underfill cannot confirm; overfill consumes real stock and lowers margin) -> the portion joins the basket for the current order line and the game returns to the counter.
E. REPACK (Harina, Asukal, Asin)
In the Timbangan window choose a bin -> choose a bag size from the tray (sizes from existing portion data) -> scoop from the bin into the bag on the wooden board; the fill meter shows the target and tolerance -> confirm -> if it was for the current order line, return to the counter; if it was a prepack (Portions.prepack), stay and add it to the "Handa na" basket (it keeps quantity and age and can be sold during service).
Switching bins with an open portion: keep reservations, ask once (continue / discard) with picture options; discard uses the existing cancel path and records discarded opened portions as spoilage.
F. UTANG (Day 5+)
An eligible named regular asks for credit (A/B reply bubbles) -> Utang notebook shows balance, limit and history -> grant (Debt.grant): a debtor balance is created, NO drawer cash is added -> later repayment (Debt.repay) uses a Sukli-style tray, raises cash, lowers receivables, adds no revenue.
G. WALLET CASH-IN (Day 4+) and CASH-OUT (Day 6+)
Cash-in: Wallet.begin -> verify recipient and principal on the phone -> reserve wallet funds BEFORE accepting tender -> customer pays principal + fee in cash (staged) -> confirm commits once atomically: wallet -principal, drawer +principal +fee.
Cash-out: customer sends the principal to the store wallet -> player matches reference, amount and confirmed status (a screenshot is never enough) -> reserve a feasible denomination combination BEFORE accepting -> fee collected -> player selects the payout denominations -> confirm commits once atomically: wallet +principal, drawer -principal, plus the fee.
Any wrong recipient/amount/reference, unconfirmed transfer, insufficient funds, unavailable denominations or reused reference gives an actionable toast and no balance change. Timeout, cancel or the Day 9 outage reverses the pending service via Wallet.cancel.
H. NAVIGATION GRAPH
Counter -> Sukli | Timbangan | Shelves | Utang | Phone (the five props). Every station -> Counter via the "Back to Counter" sign, a click outside the zoomed area, or Escape rules (pause). Timbangan has two internal modes (repack, weigh) with one persistent choice bar. Utang repay and Phone payout open a Sukli-style tray overlay on top of their parent. Pause overlay: resume, save (only at a safe transaction boundary), settings, day summary, quit (confirm). Keys 1 counter, 2 shelves, 3 sukli, 4 timbangan, 5 utang, 6 phone. Locked destinations show a toast and do not switch.
I. LEAVING A VIEW WITH AN OPEN TRANSACTION
Never cancel silently. Keep the transaction open with an "open transaction" marker on the order card. Cancel only through the existing cancel commands (Retail.cancel / Wallet.cancel / Portions cancel), which release reservations.
J. FAILURE AND RECOVERY
If cash, stock and wallet cannot support another day: recovery screen with "Ulitin ang araw" (restore the morning snapshot) and "Bagong tindahan" (new run, with confirm). This also works when the only money left is in the wallet.
