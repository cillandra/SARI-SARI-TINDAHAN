# Playing Sari-Sari Tindahan

## Preparation

Choose a category and product, then buy its supplier bundle or adjust its price. Purchases use actual drawer cash. The barya exchange obtains smaller pieces without increasing your total cash.

Sugar bags and oil bottles can be prepared before opening. Choose a supported quantity or P10/P20 option, **hold to fill**, release, and seal at the displayed target. Preparation is untimed. Open store when ready.

## Serve a neighbor

Click **Serve this neighbor** on the counter. Read the top-right order ticket; click to pin it, then Close or Escape to dismiss it.

- **Cabinet:** click the requested goods. Hover to inspect product, stock, and price.
- **Timbangan:** five visible bins contain Bigas, Harina, Asukal, Asin, and Mantika. Select the requested bin and hold **Hold to fill** or the bin/product control. Filling accelerates while held. Release in the green range and confirm.
- **Container choices:** repacking offers 1/4 kg, 1/2 kg, P10, and P20; oil offers 50 ml, 100 ml, P10, and P20. During service these choose container capacity; the quoted order stays fixed.
- **Ulitin:** restart the open portion. Opened service contents are discarded rather than returned as stock. If oil passes the spill limit, filling stops, stock is lost, and the configured P1 penalty applies; press Ulitin before trying again.
- **Switching bins:** Continue portion resumes it; Discard portion removes only that unfinished portion and preserves other basket lines.
- **TindaPay:** available from Day 2. Select cash_in or cash_out. Compare amount, recipient, reference, and confirmed status between customer evidence and store record; accept matching evidence or reject a mismatch. Cash-out requires enough drawer cash.

Return to the counter or cabinet to finish the basket. Leaving a station preserves an unfinished service reservation. Releasing mouse or Enter/Space, changing views, pausing, or losing focus stops filling.

## Receive payment

Complete every requested line, then click **Receive payment**.

Exact payment shows **Eksaktong bayad!** and finishes the customer automatically. For non-exact payment, select actual coins/bills in **Sukli**; **Undo piece** removes the last selected piece. Use the displayed total, tender, and selected cash to calculate change, then confirm once.

The first two Day 1 customers require change. Later customers can pay exact or non-exact amounts; the chance of change increases over the day plan.

Cancel sale releases reservations and staged cash and dismisses the customer. A timeout also clears the unfinished transaction. Close arrivals stops new arrivals and lets the queue finish.

## Reports, saving, and pause

Reports display sales, fees, tips, profit, drawer cash, wallet balance, losses, Suki, and stars. Next morning continues through Day 10 into endless play. Missing the daily goal alone does not end the run; Suki reaching zero does.

**Save shop** works with no active transaction. Successful purchases/barya exchanges and reports also checkpoint. Next morning saves before advancing. On a write failure, read the warning and retry Save shop. Loading the last-good backup displays a recovery warning and restores the saved paused state.

Escape or the hamburger opens Pause. Resume continues; Menu keeps the run available through Resume shop. Settings provide reduced motion and text size. There is no connected audio playback or alternate difficulty balancing.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Counter |
| 2 | Cabinet |
| 3 | Sukli |
| 4 | Timbangan |
| 5 | TindaPay, when unlocked |
| Tab / Enter | Select / activate a button |
| Escape | Close ticket/settings or toggle pause |
| F1 | Toggle runtime statistics |

Disabled controls do not activate. Ticket, pause, and bin-switch dialogs block underlying input. The game uses a letterboxed 1280x720 design canvas when resized.
